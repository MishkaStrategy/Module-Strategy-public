# Wave Classification and Market Structure

Wave legs are classified from completed information only. Inputs include ATR-normalized amplitude, path/body efficiency, relative volume, relation to the previous leg, and relation to the previous same-direction leg.

Categories: impulse, correction, expansion, contraction, exhaustion, range leg, and unclassified. Every result carries confidence, reason codes, and calculation inputs; categories do not imply trade direction or profitability.

Confirmed pivots produce HH, HL, LH, LL, equal-high/equal-low, range, or undefined labels. The current active segment is summarized as bullish, bearish, range, transition, possible reversal, or undefined, with explicit structure-break, continuation, and invalidation context.

All comparisons are segment-local. A data gap starts a new segment, and pre-gap pivots/waves cannot determine post-gap labels or ratios.
