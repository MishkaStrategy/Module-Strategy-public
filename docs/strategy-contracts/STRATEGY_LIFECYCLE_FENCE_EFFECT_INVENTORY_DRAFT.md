## Fence contract proposal

The separate Strategy Lifecycle owner is the sole authority for per-instance governance state, lifecycle revision, risk gate, qualification, and command history. Trading Runner remains the execution/runtime owner. Risk Supervisor remains authoritative for its current safety verdict. Control Plane remains a read-only projection.

Every effect writer must consult the same owner-backed fence at the actual effect boundary. A read-only `is_suspended()` call before a later side effect is insufficient: SUSPEND and submission can race. The accepted protocol must atomically order SUSPEND against effect authorization and ensure the final executor validates the fence revision or equivalent owner-issued capability. The mechanism may use a shared transaction, fencing token, or another owner-selected protocol; this draft does not choose one.

The effect decision must distinguish at least these classes:

| Effect class | Behavior while SUSPENDED | Required evidence before effect |
|---|---|---|
| New risk / entry / increase / reversal | Deny | Current lifecycle identity, incarnation and fence revision; independently verified effect class and plan/package binding |
| Pending-entry cancellation | Allow a durable, identity-scoped cancellation intent under the owner-selected policy | An authoritative, order- and instance-bound terminal/reconciled outcome is required before clearing pending state or releasing reservation; request ACK/timeout is not completion |
| Close / reduce / protection update | Continue only where owner policy classifies it as protective or reduce-only | Trusted order/position ownership and an independent action classifier/verifier; caller labels alone are insufficient |
| Fill or other factual execution event | Always ingest and reconcile the fact; do not suppress an already executed fill | Authoritative execution event identity and deduplication; preserve resulting exposure/reservation and trigger allowed protection/reconciliation |
| Recovery, replay, prepared-cycle continuation | Reconcile facts and protection; recheck the fence before any new-risk effect | Current owner revision plus authoritative source state; never restore or replay a stale permit as current |
| Runtime lifecycle hook / compensation | Classify each outbound side effect separately | Typed effect identity and policy; do not assume hooks or rollback callbacks are effect-free |

The request and outcome should eventually bind stable strategy-instance identity plus incarnation, lifecycle revision, effect/command id, target plan/order/position identity, canonical payload digest, effect class, and source evidence references. These are contract fields to resolve, not an accepted schema. The authoritative sources, authentication, signatures, trust roots, retention, and freshness rules remain unselected; no receipt is trusted merely because it has this shape.

SUSPEND must durably close the risk gate and advance its revision before cancellation side effects begin. Uncertain cancellation, late fills, stale evidence, missing identity, unavailable authority, or ambiguous persistence must preserve the closed gate and unresolved exposure/reservation until authoritative reconciliation. Restart, Runner reset, restore, or checkpoint rollback must never clear or lower the owner's fence high-water value.

Keep immutable identity separate from the execution fence epoch. Restart/recovery of the same owner-issued instance may preserve its `strategy_instance_id` and incarnation, but a new Runner/process taking over must receive a strictly newer durable fence epoch; final effect admission rejects stale epochs. Store rollback or checkpoint restore must not lower that epoch. This is a proposed contract, not a capability of the current Runner store: `RuntimeStateStore` exposes only load/save, and the existing operator lifecycle contract assumes one process without CAS or lease. The owner selected that replacement is forbidden until the prior instance's open orders and positions are authoritatively reconciled flat; no drain/transfer exception is selected. Replacement must not inherit prior orders or positions.

Owner-selected policy: recovery of the same owner-issued instance preserves identity/incarnation; a clone or parallel instance receives a new identity. Supersession links old and new records without reusing identities or rewriting history. A late fill after supersession still belongs to the old instance and must be booked/reconciled under that identity. The identity must flow through plan canonicalization, Runner dispatch, Paper order/position ownership, factual event attribution, checkpoints, and recovery; storing it only in audit metadata does not fence an effect. Independent positions for strategies can share portfolio capital if instance ownership remains explicit and shared allocation/reservations stay portfolio-scoped.

RESUME is a separate explicit owner command with compare-and-swap against the current lifecycle revision and exact strategy/package identity. The owner selected direct canonical-owner reads at RESUME plus a commit-time currentness recheck; missing sources fail closed. Ordinary RESUME reuses audit/PAPER admission only while current and unrevoked, alongside fresh plan/data/protection checks; repeat admission is required after revoke/expiry or for a new package. RESUME governance target is `Running` (authorized/bound). Concrete source identities/verifiers, trusted operator/auth and binding authority, re-binding order when current binding is absent, and implementation remain open.
