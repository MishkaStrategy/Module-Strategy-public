Replacement is forbidden until open orders and positions are authoritatively reconciled flat. These policy choices do not select concrete identity issuance, trust, evidence, or implementation mechanics.

**Owner-selected follow-up (2026-10-25):** `Running` means authorized/bound; observed Runner status is separate. RESUME uses direct canonical-owner reads and repeats currentness checks at commit. Ordinary RESUME reuses previous audit/PAPER admission only while current and unrevoked, plus fresh plan/data/protection checks; repeat admission is required after revoke/expiry or for a new package. ## Ordering and race sequences to specify

These sequences state required ordering questions; the owner-selected SQLite design does not establish current enforcement or complete protocol semantics.

1. **SUSPEND versus concurrent risk submit**
   - Runner begins an entry submit using lifecycle revision `r` while the owner receives SUSPEND.
   - The owner commits SUSPEND and closes the common risk fence at revision `r+1` before any entry-cancellation side effect.
   - The approved protocol must define its linearization point at final effect acceptance and reject stale authority there. Persistence of a submit intent or possession of an earlier permit alone does not prove that the submit linearized before SUSPEND. Outstanding pre-SUSPEND intents are subject to the same final-boundary protocol.
   - The submit is either accepted at the defined final boundary before SUSPEND takes effect and then reconciled/cancelled under policy, or it observes the closed fence and is denied. A stale pre-check cannot authorize later acceptance.
   - If commit outcome or submit outcome is ambiguous, preserve the closed fence and reconcile authoritative order/fill facts; do not infer a winner from local memory.

2. **RESUME versus evidence change / close-protection completion**
   - Operator requests RESUME with expected lifecycle revision `r`; plan/data/protection and position evidence are checked.
   - A protection/close effect or source update completes, changes evidence, or is still in flight before RESUME commits.
   - At commit, the owner must compare the exact bound evidence versions/currentness and resolve in-flight close/protection effects according to owner policy. If any bound evidence changed, expired, was revoked, or completion status is uncertain, reject RESUME and leave the fence closed. Only a successful explicit commit against still-current evidence may re-open new-risk admission.
   - The source authorities, allowed in-flight conditions, and version/freshness rules are owner decisions; engineering must not invent a local epoch to mask the gap.
