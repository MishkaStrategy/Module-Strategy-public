# External Execution Owner/Admin Decision Packet

**Status:** local request template for accountable owner and administrator responses. No unknown value below is selected or inferred. The packet records questions and the evidence needed to verify answers; it does not establish deployment, trust, or acceptance.

The selected architecture separates protected admission/controller authority, disposable execution, a separate publisher, and independent verification. The selected topology is Linux/VDS PAPER deployment, mandatory separate Darwin acceptance, and a distinct historical-backtester executor class. Operational identities, hosts, keys, groups, trust sources, and evidence storage remain unselected. Record values by reference to controlled configuration artifacts; do not place secrets, private keys, tokens, recovery codes, or credential material in this document.

## Owner/admin decisions and evidence

For every row, the accountable owner/admin must provide the exact value or controlled artifact reference, identify the approving role, and supply the listed evidence. “Independent verifier” means a reviewer or verifier outside the candidate-controlled workflow and the component being verified. If a field is not applicable, record an explicit owner-approved rationale; do not silently omit it.

## Acceptance sequence

1. Owners/admins complete the decision fields and link controlled, non-secret artifacts; unresolved fields remain explicitly **unselected**.
2. Independent reviewers verify authority, identities, configuration digests, isolation, custody, storage/replay behavior, and predecessor/root records; record verifier identity, method, exact subject digest, and result.
3. Resolve discrepancies and rejected evidence under the relevant owner; do not infer approval from a passing local test or a document’s existence.
4. Only after the required evidence is accepted may a separate, explicit authority decision consider protected CI/dispatch, publication, deployment, or release. This packet itself grants none of those permissions.

## Hold and authority boundary

No choice is inferred by this packet. It does **not** authorize Actions or CI, protected dispatch, workload execution, publication, deployment, merge, push, or release. No owner data, secret, or live credential belongs here. Keep Actions disabled and all trust/acceptance/release gates fail-closed until owners/admins provide and independent reviewers accept the corresponding evidence.
